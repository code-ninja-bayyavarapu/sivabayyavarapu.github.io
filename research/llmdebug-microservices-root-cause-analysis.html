LLMDebug: Prompt-Engineered Large Language Models for Automated Root Cause Analysis in Microservices Architectures

Tejas Pravinbhai Patel · Siva Rama Krishna Varma Bayyavarapu · Vinay Soni · Rajesh Purushothaman · Gajendra Babu Thokala · Viswanathan Ranganathan

View official record Download BibTeX

Abstract

Debugging failures in microservices is difficult because execution is distributed across services and operational evidence is spread across large volumes of logs and telemetry. LLMDebug combines prompt-engineered large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, and multi-agent analysis to identify root causes across distributed systems. The framework was evaluated on a microservices testbed spanning common failure scenarios and compared with established diagnostic baselines. The results demonstrate improved diagnostic accuracy and a substantial reduction in mean time to resolution, offering a practical approach to AI-assisted production operations.
