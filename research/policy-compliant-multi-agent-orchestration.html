Safe and Policy-Compliant Multi-Agent Orchestration for Enterprise AI

Vinil Pasupuleti · Shyalendar Reddy Allala · Siva Rama Krishna Varma Bayyavarapu · Shrey Tyagi

View official record Download BibTeX

Abstract

Enterprise AI increasingly coordinates multiple agents across workflows that must satisfy policy constraints, risk limits, and audit requirements. This work presents CAMCO, a deployment-time coordination layer that combines constraint projection, adaptive risk-weighted utility shaping, and bounded iterative negotiation. The design is compatible with existing agent architectures and can integrate with production policy engines. Evaluation across enterprise scenarios demonstrates that the framework can preserve useful agent behavior while enforcing policy-compliant actions and maintaining auditable coordination.
